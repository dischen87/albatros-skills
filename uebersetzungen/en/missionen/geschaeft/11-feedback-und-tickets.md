## What it's about

A parcel isn't delivered when it's on the van, but when it's on the doorstep. Then you ring the bell. A piece of feedback is only done once the fix is visible on your page and the person knows about it.

## What you'll learn

- to collect feedback in one place and sort it by page
- to check every report on the real page, not just against the text
- why “done in the code” doesn't yet mean done
- to write a short, friendly reply without technical talk

## How you go about it

1. Gather all the recent feedback with your AI.
2. Group it by page and rate it on the real page.
3. Have one group after the other fixed and published.
4. Check yourself whether the fix is visible.
5. Let the person know, and only then close the ticket.

## Done when

- `docs/feedback.md` with tickets, replies and one round is in your project.
