## What it's about

The floor is your cost per sale; below it you lose money. The ceiling is what your offer is worth to your customers; above it nobody buys. Your price sits in between. And at the start you offer one single dish, not a whole menu.

## What you'll learn

- How to make your offer clear in one sentence: what's included and what isn't
- How to work out what a sale costs you, your time included
- How to set a price based on value and test it honestly
- Why you have to check whether you need to charge VAT

## How you go about it

1. With your AI, you describe your smallest offer.
2. Together you work out the floor: materials, fees, your time and a share of your fixed costs.
3. You estimate the ceiling: what do your customers do today, and what does it cost them?
4. You set a price and tell it to real people from your target group.
5. You check whether you'll become liable for VAT, and record everything in `docs/angebot.md`.

## Done when

- the file `docs/angebot.md` with your offer, calculation and price is in your project.
