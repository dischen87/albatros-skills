## What it's about

Your brand is your shop sign in a busy lane. It includes the name, the way you talk and the character people sense at first glance. And the sign mustn't look like the shop next door. That's why you check your name before you paint it on a sign. Colours and type then grow out of the character, in the next step.

## What you'll learn

- How to say in three sentences what you stand for
- How to find a tone that suits you and your customers
- How to collect names and check whether they're free: trademark register, Handelsregister (Swiss commercial register), domain
- Why at the start your name in a nice typeface is enough as a logo
- Whether a mascot belongs to your business: yes, no or later

## How you go about it

1. With your AI, you write down the core of your brand and your tone.
2. Together you collect names and make a shortlist.
3. You check the names on Swissreg, in the Handelsregister and for the domain, and do a say-it-out-loud test with real people.
4. You settle the character in three words, say what has to stay and whether a mascot belongs to it.
5. Everything goes into `docs/konzept.md` with a date, the reasoning with the checks into `docs/marke.md`.

## Done when

- the file `docs/marke.md` with core, tone, name and checks is in your project
- `docs/konzept.md` has all its lines and says yes, no or later for the mascot
- and the “Concept” step is reached on your island.
