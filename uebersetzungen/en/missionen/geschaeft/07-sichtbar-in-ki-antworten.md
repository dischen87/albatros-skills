## What it's about

These days, many people ask an AI before they buy something. The AI is like the information desk at the station: it names the place that answers the question most precisely and most up to date. Shouting doesn't help there. A clear, dated notice does.

## What you'll learn

- which questions your customers ask an AI without knowing your name
- whether search robots are even allowed onto your page (the house rules `robots.txt`)
- how to measure honestly instead of guessing
- which page to make more precise first

## How you go about it

1. Collect 10 to 20 real customer questions with your AI, and then don't change them any more.
2. Have it check whether search robots are allowed to read your page, and able to.
3. Ask the questions in two or three AI services and record the answers.
4. Look at who gets named instead of you, and why.
5. Make one page more precise and measure again after a few weeks.

## Done when

- `docs/ki-sichtbarkeit.md` with the list of questions, the technical check and the first measurement is in your project.
