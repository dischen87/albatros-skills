## What it's about

On the noticeboard at the supermarket, you understand a good notice as you walk past and tear off a slip if it fits. Your landing page is that kind of notice on the web: one page, one offer, one action. And the missing slips tell you whether it works. In this step you plan it: its build plan goes into your floor plan.

## What you'll learn

- Why a page needs exactly one main thing
- What belongs in the first screen you see without scrolling
- How buttons and texts state the result in everyday words
- How a five-second test shows whether strangers understand you

## How you go about it

1. With your AI, you settle the one action visitors should take.
2. Together you plan the home page from top to bottom and write the texts in your customers' words.
3. You settle which completed action gets counted and how you'll know the page is finished.
4. You plan the five-second test: as soon as there's a draft to click through, you show the first screen to a few people from your target group for five seconds and improve whatever is unclear.
5. Everything goes into your plan `docs/plan.md`. The page only gets built once you've approved the draft for building.

## Done when

- the build plan of your home page from top to bottom and the acceptance checks are in `docs/plan.md`
- and the “Plan” step is reached on your island.
