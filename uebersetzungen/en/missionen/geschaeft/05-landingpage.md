## What it's about

On the noticeboard at the supermarket, you understand a good notice as you walk past and tear off a slip if it fits. Your landing page is that kind of notice on the web: one page, one offer, one action. And the missing slips tell you whether it works.

## What you'll learn

- Why a page needs exactly one main thing
- What belongs in the first screen you see without scrolling
- How buttons and texts state the result in everyday words
- How a five-second test shows whether strangers understand you

## How you go about it

1. With your AI, you settle the one action visitors should take.
2. Together you plan the page from top to bottom and write the texts in your customers' words.
3. Your AI builds the page, and every completed action is counted.
4. You show the first screen to a few people from your target group for five seconds and improve whatever is unclear.
5. The page goes live, and you check it together at the real address.
