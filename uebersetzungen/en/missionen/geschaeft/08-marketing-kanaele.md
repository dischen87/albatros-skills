## What it's about

Your business has many doors: web search, recommendations, social media, partners, flyers. You want to know which door the buyers come through, not just the window-shoppers. For that, every link gets a tag, like a suitcase at the airport.

## What you'll learn

- Which ways there are to reach customers, and how to pick two or three of them
- How to give links a tag (UTM) so your statistics know where visitors came from
- Why you test one channel after the other
- Why enquiries and purchases count, not visits or followers

## How you go about it

1. With your AI, you list all the doors and choose the most promising ones.
2. Your AI makes you a tagged link for each channel, and for flyers a QR code too.
3. You plan the first test in writing: duration, budget and how you'll recognise success.
4. You open one door and leave the others as they are.
5. At the end you compare enquiries and costs together and decide: keep, change or drop.

## Done when

- the file `docs/kanaele.md` with your channels, links and the first test plan is in your project.
