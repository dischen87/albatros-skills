## What it's about

Before every take-off, the pilot walks once round the plane and ticks off a fixed list, on the real plane, not in the manual. In the same way, you check your site at its real address before the flyers are printed. The address has been in `docs/lancieren.md` since you chose where your site lives. And the flight recorder, your statistics, is already running at take-off.

## What you'll learn

- to check the live offer instead of the code or your memory
- what a blocker is and what can wait until after take-off
- why you can't measure your first visitors after the fact
- how to see after every fix that it's really right

## How you go about it

1. Go through the checklist with your AI on your real site, at the address from `docs/lancieren.md`.
2. Try out the form, the emails and, if there is one, the checkout yourself.
3. Sort every finding: blocker, first days, first week or later.
4. Fix the blockers and check each one again.
5. Only tell others once it says “Start: yes”. A few days after the start, go through it again.

If your site lives with Albatros, Albatros checks this mission through your logbook (in the harbour or on your server). If you host it yourself, that's not possible: you still do the pre-flight check, Albatros just can't tick it off for you.

## Done when

- `docs/go-live-check.md` with a summary and findings is in your project
- and the step “Live” is reached on your island, so your site has been checked from outside at its address.
