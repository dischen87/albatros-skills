## What it's about

Nobody builds a house without a floor plan. Before your AI builds a single line, the two of you clear things up: Who is the page for? What should visitors do? Which pages does it need? What's already there? One sheet of paper is enough and saves a lot of rebuilding.

## What you'll learn

- to describe your page in one sentence
- to settle on one single main thing for visitors
- to start with five pages at most
- how you'll tell later whether the page is working

## How you go about it

1. Your AI asks you eight questions, one after the other.
2. Together you write down the answers as a floor plan.
3. You read the plan and say whether it's right.
4. The plan goes into the logbook and onto the server.

## Done when

- the file `docs/plan.md` is in your project.
