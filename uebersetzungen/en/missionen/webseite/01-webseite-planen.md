## What it's about

Nobody builds a house without a floor plan. After the idea and the research, your AI draws the floor plan of your site with you: which pages it needs, what visitors can do, who may see what, in which languages, by when and who looks after it later. One sheet of paper is enough and saves a lot of rebuilding.

## What you'll learn

- to start with few pages: five to seven at most, counting the legal notice and privacy policy
- why every page has to be looked after
- that every feature needs a flow with a confirmation and a case for when something goes wrong
- how you'll both know the site is finished

## How you go about it

1. Your AI asks you at most five questions that only you can answer, at most two at a time: what visitors can do, who may see what, in which languages, by when and who looks after the site later.
2. It suggests the rest with a reason: the pages, whether you need a building block like a sign-up list, and how you'll know the site is finished. You confirm these assumptions in one message.
3. Everything goes into `docs/plan.md` as a floor plan. Whatever nobody knows yet is marked as open there; nothing gets made up.
4. Your AI reports the “Plan” step on your island. The plan stays on your computer until your site goes live.

## Done when

- the “Plan” step is reached on your island. Your AI reports it as soon as `docs/plan.md` in your project has all its lines.
