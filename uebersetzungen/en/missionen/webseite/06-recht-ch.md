## What it's about

The legal notice (Impressum) is your doorbell nameplate: who lives here? The privacy policy is the house rules for visitors: what happens to what they give you or leave behind? You put both in order for your Swiss website. This doesn't replace legal advice, but it covers the basic duties.

## What you'll learn

- who needs a legal notice and what goes into it
- what the DSG (Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection) requires of your page
- when cookies need a notice or consent
- why marketing emails are only allowed with consent

## How you go about it

1. Write down which data your page collects and where it goes.
2. Write the legal notice.
3. Write a privacy policy that fits your page.
4. Link both in the footer of every page.
