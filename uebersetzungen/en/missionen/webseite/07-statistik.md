## What it's about

A shop owner at the door only counts what's on her list beforehand, and only once it has really happened. She doesn't remember faces. That's exactly how you set up the statistics for your website: a few clear tally marks, at the right moment, privacy-friendly.

## What you'll learn

- what an event is and when it should count
- one goal event for what matters to you
- statistics without cookies
- to filter out your own visits and check the numbers against reality

## How you go about it

1. Write a small event plan.
2. Create a PostHog project with its location in the EU.
3. Add it to your page, with the cookieless setting.
4. Fire the goal event after success, and test it.

## Done when

- the event plan `docs/statistik.md` is in your project
- and your page contains the PostHog snippet.
