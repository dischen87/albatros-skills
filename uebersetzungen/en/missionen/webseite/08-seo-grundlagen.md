## What it's about

A search engine is a city map that a messenger redraws every day. For your shop to appear on it, you need a clear sign above the door, a site plan for the messenger and signposts from other places to you.

## What you'll learn

- what your target group searches for
- page title, description and main heading
- what `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt` are
- what local businesses need on top

## How you go about it

1. Write down five phrases your target group searches for.
2. Give every page a title, a description and one main heading.
3. Set up the site plan and house rules for the messenger.
4. Check the loading time on a phone.
5. With your own domain: register the page with the search engine.

## Done when

- `https://{name}.aufwind.sh/sitemap.xml` and `https://{name}.aufwind.sh/robots.txt` answer.
