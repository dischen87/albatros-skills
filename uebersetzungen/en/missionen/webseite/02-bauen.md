## What it's about

A house goes up in a fixed order: first a sketch, then views to choose from, then the building plan with a signature, then the building work, then moving in. Your AI builds your page the same way, step by step, and the building only starts once you've approved the plan. On your island in the cockpit, your house grows with every step.

## What you'll learn

- to look at a sketch (mockup) and drafts and choose
- what a building plan (PRD) is and why you approve it before anything is built
- to look at your page on your own computer first
- how an entry in the logbook gets onto the web via the harbour

## How you go about it

1. Your AI shows you a sketch of the home page and then two or three drafts. You pick one.
2. It writes the building plan on one page. You read it and say yes or what's still missing.
3. It builds the page and shows it to you in the browser, at phone and at computer width.
4. It sends everything to the harbour. Shortly after, your page is on the web at your address.

## Done when

- the file `docs/prd.md` is in your project and
- your page answers at `https://{name}.aufwind.sh`.
