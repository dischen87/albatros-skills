## What it's about

New paint over sticking doors only makes the faults look prettier. That's why you first check with your AI whether your page is easy to use, and repair that. Then you choose from three clearly different directions and paint everything with a fixed colour fan.

## What you'll learn

- to check a page from three angles: flows, access, states and language
- criticism with evidence instead of taste
- what sets directions apart (not just the colour)
- fixed values for colours, type and spacing (tokens)

## How you go about it

1. Check, and write down findings with evidence.
2. Decide, and repair how the page works.
3. Look at three directions on the same pages and choose one.
4. Settle the colour fan and show it on a sample sheet: colours, fonts, buttons and fields.
5. Look at the sample sheet on your phone and on your computer.

## Done when

- you've chosen a direction and seen the sample sheet
- and the “Drafts” step is reached on your island.
