## What it's about

Stairs work well for many people, but for some they're a wall. A ramp next to them helps those people, and everyone else is happy to use it too. You check your page for ramps: enough contrast, descriptions for pictures, use without a mouse, clear forms.

## What you'll learn

- what automatic checking tools find and what they don't
- to calculate contrast instead of guessing it
- to use your page with the keyboard only
- what your page sounds like when it's read aloud

## How you go about it

1. Run an automatic first check.
2. Check by hand: seeing, using, reading aloud, forms, motion.
3. Write everything into a check report.
4. Repair, most important points first, and check again.

## Done when

- the check report `docs/barrierefreiheit.md` is in your project.
