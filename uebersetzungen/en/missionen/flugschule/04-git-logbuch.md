## What it's about

Every ship has a logbook: who did what, when, and why? Git is that logbook for your project. Every entry records a state you can go back to. A copy is kept in the harbour, at Albatros on `code.aufwind.sh`. Even if your computer falls in the water, nothing is lost.

## What you'll learn

- what an entry in the logbook is (commit) and what a good note for it looks like
- how the copy gets to the harbour (push)
- why you only enter your own changes

## How you go about it

1. Have a look around the harbour: your logbook is already there, with the template in it.
2. Fetch the logbook onto your computer.
3. Change something, enter it, send it to the harbour. Twice.
4. Check whether the entries have also arrived on your server, in the folder `~/projekt`.

## Done when

- your logbook has at least two entries on `main`: with Light in the harbour, with Pilot also in `~/projekt` on your server.
