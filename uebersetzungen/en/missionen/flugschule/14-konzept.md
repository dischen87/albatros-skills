## What it's about

A house has a character before it gets its colours: cosy, clear or playful. Choose the colours first and you end up painting a house nobody wanted that way. So for your site, you and your AI first settle what it's called, how it sounds and how it should come across. Colours and fonts come after that.

## What you'll learn

- character before colours
- your core in three sentences: what you promise and why you of all people
- formal or informal, and how your texts sound
- whether a character guides people through your site: yes, no or later

## How you go about it

1. You decide whether the project name stays or whether your project goes by a different name to the outside world.
2. You choose the tone and three words that describe how your site should come across.
3. You say what has to stay: logo, house colour or font.
4. Your AI suggests the core and the visual style, and you confirm or change them.
5. Everything goes into `docs/konzept.md`. Your AI only makes pictures with the Albatros image tools if it has told you the price first and you say yes.

On the business path, your brand is added: checking the name, the tone and a simple logo.

## Done when

- `docs/konzept.md` has all its lines and says yes, no or later for the mascot
- and the “Concept” step is reached on your island.
