## What it's about

The shell is up and checked. Now you walk through it yourself: on your computer, at phone width and at computer width. Your AI compares every page with the draft and shows you every difference. Along the way, you change a text yourself and see the change straight away: that's how you notice your site is made of files you can touch yourself. At the end, you release the site. It only goes online when going live.

## What you'll learn

- looking at your site on your own computer, at phone width and at computer width, and comparing it with the draft
- changing a text yourself and seeing the change straight away
- keeping a change (an entry in the logbook) or undoing it
- why only your release opens the way onto the web

## How you go about it

1. Your AI starts the preview of your site on your computer and opens it for you.
2. You look at every page, at phone width and at computer width. Your AI compares them with the draft and shows you every difference.
3. You open the file of a page, change a sentence and save it. Your AI builds again, you reload the preview and see your change. In learning mode it's a must, otherwise it's an offer.
4. You decide: keep it, and an entry goes into the logbook, or go back to the earlier version.
5. You release the site. It only goes onto the web after that, when going live, never without your release.

## Done when

- the step “Release” is reached on your island. Your AI only reports it once your release is in `docs/prd.md` (`Freigegeben:`, the word stays German).
