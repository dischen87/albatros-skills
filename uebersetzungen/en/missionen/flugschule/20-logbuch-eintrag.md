## What it's about

Your draft is approved for building. Now your AI builds the real site from it on your own computer, with the same pages, menus and sections as the draft. Like on a ship, a logbook records every step: each entry (a commit) gets a short note saying what and why. You don't need a harbour for that yet, the logbook lives in your project folder. At the end, four checks inspect the shell. Up to here everything is free, and nothing goes online yet.

## What you'll learn

- what an entry in the logbook (a commit) is, and why every step gets one
- what a good note sounds like: what and why, in one sentence
- how your draft turns into the building plan (PRD) and then the real site, without anything shifting
- how four checks (security, flows and access, looks, code and speed) inspect a site, and which doors stay closed without signing in

## How you go about it

1. Your AI checks that your draft is approved for building, and opens the logbook on your computer.
2. You make the first entry together: your AI shows you what goes into it, and you type the `git commit` command yourself. In learning mode, that's part of it.
3. It writes the way of building and the building plan (PRD) from your draft, and suggests which addresses are open without signing in. You confirm both in one message.
4. It builds the site and checks it in four areas. It shows you the reports with their traffic lights.
5. Every step you finish gets an entry in the logbook.

## Done when

- your logbook has an entry with a note for every step
- and the “Checks” step is reached on your island.
