## What it's about

Your server is a flat in a big building: your own door, your own rooms, your own appliances. The terminal is the intercom. On a tour you learn the most important phrases for finding your way around.

## What you'll learn

- where you're standing right now (`pwd`) and what's here (`ls`)
- how to go from room to room (`cd`)
- how much space and work surface you have (`df`, `free`)
- which appliances are running right now (`docker ps`)
- why `sudo` is the caretaker's master key

## How you go about it

1. Go in with `ssh {name}@aufwind.sh`.
2. Type the tour commands yourself; your AI explains every answer.
3. Write down three things you discovered.

## Done when

- the file `~/rundgang.md` with your notes is on your server.
