## What it's about

At an opening, a ribbon hangs across the door. It only gets cut when everything is ready, and then there should be guests too. For your site, that means: your AI sends off exactly the site you released, checks it from outside at its address, and you tell the first people.

## What you'll learn

- why only the released site goes onto the web
- how your logbook gets onto the web via the harbour
- only what's been checked from outside counts as live
- your own address (domain): now, later or never
- who you tell first

## How you go about it

1. Your AI checks that your release is in `docs/prd.md`. Without it, nothing goes.
2. It sends your site to the harbour. With Light it's then on the web; with Pilot the conveyor belt delivers it. If you host it yourself, it goes to your provider.
3. It checks your site from outside at its address, including the legal notice and privacy policy.
4. You decide whether you want your own address: now, later or never.
5. You say who you'll tell first. Your AI notes everything in `docs/live.md`.

On the business path, you take one walk round the plane before you tell anyone: the pre-flight check on your real site. If you're modernising an existing site, your AI switches the domain over with redirects for the old addresses.

## Done when

- your site answers at its address with no red and `docs/live.md` has all its lines
- and the “Live” step is reached on your island.
