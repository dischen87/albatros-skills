## What it's about

Anyone flying to a country they don't know reads the travel guide first: what people expect there, what to avoid, what to bring. For your site, the travel guides are comparable sites in your region and line of business. Your AI looks for up to three, opens each one and shows you what they have in common and what you could do differently.

## What you'll learn

- taking inspiration is allowed, copying isn't
- what visitors expect from sites like yours
- which obligations apply: legal notice, privacy, image rights, cookies
- why missing texts and photos are the most common reason websites never get finished

## How you go about it

1. You name sites you like. Your AI looks for up to three comparable ones and opens every address. No made-up addresses.
2. Together you write down what's usual, what you'll adopt and what you'll deliberately do differently.
3. Your AI works out which obligations apply to your site.
4. You say which texts, photos and which logo you already have.
5. Everything goes into `docs/recherche.md`, including the words someone would use to search for you.

On the business path, your target group plus offer and price are added. If you're modernising an existing site, this step is the inventory: what does the old site have that must stay?

## Done when

- `docs/recherche.md` has all its lines, every example with an address your AI has opened,
- and the “Research” step is reached on your island.
