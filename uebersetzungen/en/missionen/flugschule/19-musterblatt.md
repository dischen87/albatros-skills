## What it's about

Before anything gets painted, the house has to stand. That's why your AI first shows you a grey mockup: your home page without colours, just with the order of the sections. It asks: is the order right? Then it puts three clearly different directions in front of you, grown from the character in your concept, and you choose. Your choice turns into a fixed colour fan and a sample sheet where you see colours, fonts, buttons and fields at a glance. From then on, colours and fonts live in one place only: the colour fan.

## What you'll learn

- structure before looks
- what sets directions apart (not just the colour)
- fixed values for colours, type and spacing in one place (tokens)
- why a colour changes everywhere when you change it in the colour fan
- why contrasts get recalculated rather than estimated

## How you go about it

1. Look at the grey mockup and say whether the order is right.
2. Compare three directions on the same page and choose one, or mix them.
3. Say whether you want design elements: icons, illustrations, photos, a character of your own.
4. Your AI settles the colour fan, light and dark, with recalculated contrasts.
5. Look at the sample sheet on your phone and on your computer and say whether it works.
6. Change a colour in the colour fan yourself (`design/tokens.css`), reload the sample sheet and watch it change everywhere. In learning mode, that's part of it.

## Done when

- you've chosen a direction and seen the sample sheet
- and the “Drafts” step is reached on your island.
