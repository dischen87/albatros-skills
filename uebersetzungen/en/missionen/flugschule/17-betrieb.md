## What it's about

Before the first guests arrive, the letterbox is up, the smoke alarm beeps when there's smoke, and the front door has a lock. For your site, that means: visitors can get in touch and someone reads it, you find out when the site stops responding, and you know how to get back to yesterday. You set this up now, before the site goes live.

## What you'll learn

- how visitors get in touch with you, and who reads it how often
- who finds out when the site stops responding
- back to yesterday: how to undo a mistake
- whether you want to count who comes, and why only with consent
- when you need an entrance with sign-in (only with your own server)

## How you go about it

1. You say how visitors reach you and who reads the messages.
2. Your AI suggests who gets told about an outage, and you confirm.
3. Your AI works out how to get back to yesterday and shows you.
4. You decide whether to count who comes, and whether several parts of your site need to know who's signed in.
5. Everything goes into `docs/betrieb.md`.

## Done when

- `docs/betrieb.md` has all its lines
- and the “Running it” step is reached on your island.
