## What it's about

Before the shell of a house goes up, the architect has a model you can walk through with your eyes: every room, every door. Each change still costs only a sentence. Your AI makes a draft of your whole site in the same way, in the direction you chose and with real texts. You click your way through it, on a phone and on a computer.

## What you'll learn

- right now a change is one sentence, after the build it's a rebuild
- what confirmation, error and empty states should look like
- why missing texts stay visible as [placeholder]
- why check scripts help from here on: Node, npm and Git on your computer

## How you go about it

1. Your AI checks whether Node, npm and Git are on your computer and shows you how to install whatever's missing.
2. It drafts every page from your plan in the direction you chose, with real texts. Anything still missing shows as [placeholder].
3. For every function, it shows what the confirmation, error and empty state look like. Pages of building blocks, for example for booking, are a dummy that already looks real.
4. A check script looks at whether every page is there, every link leads somewhere and only values from your colour fan are used.
5. You click your way through, on a phone and on a computer, and say what's missing or bothers you. Your AI makes changes, shows you the draft again and writes your feedback into `docs/entwurf.md`.

When everything is right, it asks you: “Shall we build it like this?” Only your clear yes approves the draft for building. Only what you've seen gets built.

## Done when

- your draft in `design/entwurf/` shows every page from your plan, all links lead to pages that exist and the check script reports nothing red
- and the “Draft” step is reached on your island.
