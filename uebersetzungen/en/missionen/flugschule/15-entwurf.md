## What it's about

Before the shell of a house goes up, the architect has a model you can walk through with your eyes: every room, every door. Each change still costs only a sentence. Your AI makes a draft of your whole site in the same way, in the direction you chose and with real texts. You click your way through it, on a phone and on a computer.

## What you'll learn

- right now a change is one sentence, after the build it's a rebuild
- what confirmation, error and empty states should look like
- why missing texts stay visible as [placeholder]
- why check scripts help from here on: Node, npm and Git on your computer

## How you go about it

1. Your AI checks whether Node, npm and Git are on your computer and helps if something's missing.
2. It drafts every page from your plan with real texts. Anything still missing shows as [placeholder].
3. For every function, it shows what the confirmation, error and empty state look like.
4. You click your way through, on a phone and on a computer, and say what's missing or bothers you.
5. Your AI makes changes until it's right and writes your feedback into `docs/entwurf.md`.

When everything is right, you then approve the draft for building. Only what you've seen gets built.

## Done when

- your draft in `design/entwurf/` shows every page from your plan and all links lead to pages that exist
- and the “Draft” step is reached on your island.
