## What it's about

Your house is planned, built and signed off. Now it needs a place to stand. Albatros has two: Light for a site without its own services, and Pilot with your own server for sites with building blocks like sign-up or login. Or you put your house somewhere yourself. You decide where it stands. Then you fetch the key.

## What you'll learn

- what sets Light, Pilot and hosting it yourself apart: building blocks, costs and how much you do yourself
- why your AI only quotes prices from your account and never from memory
- what an SSH key is: house key and lock
- why at most the template is online for now

## How you go about it

1. Your AI shows you the options, with the prices from your account.
2. You choose where your site lives. Your AI writes your choice and the reason into `docs/lancieren.md`.
3. With Albatros, your AI only opens the payment page after you've chosen. You type your card details only there, never in the chat.
4. You make your house key (SSH key) if the place where your site lives needs one. When hosting it yourself, you create the account with the provider yourself.
5. With Albatros, your AI checks the new address. At first the template is there; your site only arrives when you go live.

## Done when

- where your site lives, with the reason and the address, is in `docs/lancieren.md`
- and the “Where it lives” step is reached on your island.
