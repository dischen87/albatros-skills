## What it's about

If you write everything down, the bill never comes as a surprise. You keep an honest household budget book for your project: what costs a fixed amount every month, what gets added depending on use, and where you need a cap.

## What you'll learn

- to keep rent, utility bills and one-off purchases apart
- where costs without a cap are lurking and how to set one
- when more power or your own server is worth it

## How you go about it

1. Collect all the items with your AI.
2. Look up the current prices with the providers.
3. Mark the traps and set caps.
4. Write the household budget book into the project and set a date for the next review.

## Done when

- the file `docs/kosten.md` is in your project.
