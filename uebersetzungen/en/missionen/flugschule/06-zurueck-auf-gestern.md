## What it's about

Every night, a photo is taken of your whole flat. If something goes wrong, you bring it back just as it was in the photo. The technical term is snapshot. In this mission you break your page on purpose and repair it again. After that, you're no longer afraid of mistakes.

## What you'll learn

- what a snapshot is and when to take one yourself
- how “back to yesterday” works
- what gets lost along the way and why the logbook in the harbour still matters

## How you go about it

1. Take a photo of your own.
2. Switch off your page on purpose and see in the browser that it's gone.
3. Go back to the photo.
4. The page is back.

## Done when

- the container `webseite` is running again
- and `https://{name}.aufwind.sh` answers again.
